Delete your account

Deleting your account here is switched off for the moment.

We found that removing an account could also have removed a league you run — along with the predictions of everyone else in it. Rather than risk that, this button is off until the fix is in.

Your right to have your account deleted is unaffected. Email support@dota2tileague.com from the address you signed up with and we will remove it, along with your profile, predictions, league memberships and notification subscription.

Everything we hold and why: privacy.

Step 1 of 2 — confirm it's you

Enter the email address you signed up with. We'll send you a link. Opening that link brings you back here to confirm — the same way signing up worked, only in reverse.

Nothing is deleted until you come back and confirm. Asking for the link does not start anything.

Step 2 of 2 — confirm deletion

You're about to permanently delete the account for .

This cannot be undone. It removes:

  • your account and sign-in
  • your nickname and profile
  • every prediction you have made, and your place on every leaderboard
  • your membership of every league
  • your notification subscription

If you run a league, it is handed to whoever joined it first so the other members keep their predictions. A league with nobody left in it is deleted with your account.

Your account has been deleted.

Nothing personal remains. You're welcome back any time — you'd just sign up again.
