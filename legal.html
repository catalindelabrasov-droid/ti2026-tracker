This is not gambling

dota2tileague.com is free. There is no entry fee, no stake, no wagering and no prize of any value — no money, no goods, no credit, nothing that can be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out. Prediction leagues are private groups of friends playing for points and bragging rights only. Nothing on this site is a betting product and no betting is offered, brokered or advertised here.

What this is

A free, non-commercial, fan-made tracker for the Dota 2 tournament The International, with an optional prediction game for private groups. It is run as a hobby project. There are no ads, nothing is sold, and there is no subscription.

  • One person, one account. Do not create accounts to inflate a leaderboard.

Things you write

Nicknames, league names, team names and tournament descriptions are visible to other people. Do not use anything unlawful, hateful, harassing, sexual, or that impersonates somebody else. We can remove content or an account that breaks this, without notice. If you see something that does, tell us at the address below.

Prediction leagues

Leagues are private and joined with a code. Anyone holding the code can join, so share it only with people you want in your league. The league admin sets the scoring rules and can change them. Predictions close when a match starts and this is enforced by the server — nobody, including the admin, can predict a match that has begun.

Points have no value of any kind and cannot be transferred, exchanged or redeemed.

Where it is stored

The database and accounts run on Supabase, hosted in the European Union (Ireland). The website itself is served by Netlify, which operates a global network and keeps ordinary access logs.

Other services your browser contacts

Loading a page also contacts these, and each necessarily sees your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — the two fonts the site uses.
  • jsDelivr — a code library the site loads.
  • Steam / Valve and Liquipedia — hero, item and team images are loaded from their servers.
  • Twitch — only on the watch page, and only if you start a stream. Twitch sets its own cookies and applies its own privacy policy.
  • Your browser maker's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla) — only if you turned on notifications, since that is how a notification physically reaches your device.

Cookies

No advertising or analytics cookies are set, so there is no consent banner. The site keeps a sign-in session and a few of your own settings in your browser's local storage, which is what keeps you signed in and remembers your preferences. Clearing your browser data clears them.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Then your profile, league memberships, predictions and notification subscription are deleted permanently and immediately. Backups roll off within 30 days. Anonymous, non-personal records — for example a league's final scoreboard with no identifiable person in it — may remain.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, have it deleted, have its use restricted, object to it being used, take it elsewhere, and withdraw consent at any time. To have your account and everything in it deleted, email us from the address you signed up with and we will remove it. We answer within 30 days. See delete-account for exactly what deletion removes.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania that is the ANSPDCP — dataprotection.ro.
