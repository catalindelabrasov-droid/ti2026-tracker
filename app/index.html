Put TI League on your phone

Tournaments, the ladder and your league — full screen, one tap from the home screen.

Easiest — any phone

Install from your browser

No download, no security warning. It behaves like any other app once it's on your home screen.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. That's it.
  1. Open dota2tileague.com in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
Android only

Download the Android app

A proper installed app, handy if you want to pass it round a Discord. Android 6 and up. Can notify you when a TI match starts.

Download APK · 1.2 MB
  1. Tap the file once it's downloaded.
  2. Android will say it can't install from this source — tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source. It asks because the app didn't come from the Play Store, not because anything is wrong with it.
  3. Go back and tap Install.

It updates itself

Both versions are the website, not a copy of it. When something changes — a new format, a fixed bracket, anything on the server — it's there the next time you open the app. You will never be asked to reinstall to get the latest version.

Know when TI starts

Tap the 🔔 in the app and you'll get a notification when a match is about to begin, and again when it actually goes live — schedules slip, so we tell you both. It arrives with the app closed and your phone in your pocket. Tap the bell again to stop.
